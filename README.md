# Mongol Resurgence — an Europa Universalis V mod

*When the Yuan were driven from China in 1368 — what if the Mongol tribes had
genuinely reunified and launched a new wave of conquest westward?*

A three-phase alternate-history "railroad" from steppe unification (1368) to a
restored Yeke Mongol Ulus holding the seats of all four khanates (1650), fully
playable by AI or human, plus three standalone late-steppe situations covering
what actually happened: Ligdan Khan's Chahar bid, the Torghut migration to the
Volga, and the Dzungar Khanate.
